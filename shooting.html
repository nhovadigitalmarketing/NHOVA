SHOOTING DETAILS

Planned photo and video production that gives your brand, products, services or campaigns original visual assets to use across digital channels.

WHAT IS THIS SERVICE ABOUT

Shooting is the production stage where we capture intentional photographs or video footage for your communication goals. The work can cover a product session, brand story, campaign shoot, event or social content day.

WHY THIS SERVICE MATTERS

Original visuals show the real people, products and spaces behind a business. A considered shoot creates consistent source material instead of relying on mismatched stock images or rushed content.

BUSINESS BENEFITS

  • More credible brand and product presentation
  • A usable library of original photo and video assets
  • Fresh material for social posts, reels and campaigns
  • Visual consistency across customer touchpoints

WHAT IS INCLUDED

  • Pre-shoot brief and planning discussion
  • Shot list and visual direction
  • Production scheduling and coordination
  • Photo and/or video capture
  • Selected deliverables for agreed usage
  • Basic post-production scope as agreed

HOW NHOVA WORKS

We clarify the purpose, audience, locations, subjects, formats and deliverables first. Nhova prepares the shot plan, coordinates the shoot, captures the agreed scenes and organises the selected output for your channels.
